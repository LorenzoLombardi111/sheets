Free Spreadsheet Online with No Account or Signup

Every major online spreadsheet requires you to create an account before you can use it. Google Sheets needs a Google account. Excel Online needs a Microsoft account. Zoho Sheet needs a Zoho account. Even many smaller alternatives ask for your email address before letting you touch a cell. freesheet.app is different. It requires nothing.

Open freesheet.app in any browser and you have a fully functional spreadsheet ready for input. No account creation, no email verification, no password to remember, and no terms of service to agree to. Your data stays entirely in your browser and is never sent to any server.

Why would you want a spreadsheet with no account?

There are many legitimate reasons to avoid creating yet another online account. You might be on a shared computer and do not want to sign in to a personal account. You might be privacy-conscious and prefer not to hand your data to a cloud service. You might be in a hurry and do not want to spend two minutes on a signup form when you just need to add up a column of numbers. Or you might simply be tired of the account fatigue that comes with modern software.

freesheet.app respects all of these situations. It provides a capable spreadsheet experience with zero friction. The application loads in under 200 milliseconds, and your spreadsheet is ready the moment the page appears. There is no loading screen, no cookie consent banner, and no onboarding flow.

What features are included without an account?

Everything. freesheet.app does not gate any features behind a login or paywall. You get 10 built-in formulas including SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, IF, ROUND, ABS, CONCAT, and TODAY. You get cell formatting with bold, italic, text color, background color, and text alignment. You get CSV and Excel file import via file picker or drag-and-drop, and CSV export to save your work. You get undo and redo with 50 steps of history, multi-cell selection with live status bar statistics, resizable columns, and dark mode.

The grid provides 26 columns (A through Z) and 2,000 rows, which is more than enough for personal budgets, project tracking, data cleanup, and most everyday spreadsheet tasks.

How is your data saved without an account?

freesheet.app uses your browser's localStorage to save all cell data, formatting, column widths, and the sheet name. This storage persists between browser sessions, so when you come back to freesheet.app tomorrow, your spreadsheet will be exactly as you left it. No server, no sync, no cloud backup.

The trade-off is that your data is tied to one browser on one device. If you switch to a different computer, you will not see your previous spreadsheet. For important work, use the CSV export button to download a backup file that you can open anywhere. This is a deliberate design choice: by keeping data local, freesheet.app ensures that your information never leaves your device.

Is it really private with no tracking?

Yes. freesheet.app contains no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, no cookies, and no third-party code beyond font files from Google Fonts. There is no backend server, no database, and no API. The entire application is a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser. Your spreadsheet data exists only in localStorage on your device and is never accessible to anyone else, including the developers of freesheet.app.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit, freesheet.app is cached by a service worker and works fully offline. You can open it on a plane, on a train, or anywhere without internet. Data is saved to localStorage automatically, so nothing is lost when you are disconnected. This makes freesheet.app one of the few online spreadsheets that truly works without any network connection.
